# Orbitly Employee Handbook

Orbitly is a fictional software company used as demo data for RagForge. Every policy below is invented for testing retrieval.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation accrues monthly, at a rate of slightly more than two days per month, and new hires can use accrued days after completing 60 days of employment.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used. Requests longer than five consecutive working days must be submitted in the HR portal at least 30 days in advance and approved by the direct manager.

Orbitly also observes a company-wide winter shutdown between December 24 and January 1. These days do not count against the vacation balance.

## Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over. A medical certificate is only required when an absence lasts more than three consecutive working days.

## Remote work

Orbitly is a remote-first company. Employees can work from any country where Orbitly has a legal entity: Brazil, Portugal, and Canada. Working temporarily from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per year, as long as the employee informs the People team at least two weeks in advance.

Everyone is expected to be available during the core collaboration window from 13:00 to 16:00 UTC. Outside that window, schedules are flexible.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every three years.

Each employee also receives a one-time home office stipend of 600 USD for a chair, desk, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 90 days of the start date.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses, books, certifications, and conference tickets. Travel to conferences is paid from a separate travel budget and requires manager approval. The learning budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted in the finance tool within 30 days, together with a receipt. Expenses below 50 USD are approved automatically. Anything above 50 USD needs approval from the direct manager, and anything above 1,000 USD also needs approval from Finance.
