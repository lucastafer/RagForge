# Orbitly Notes — Product FAQ

Orbitly Notes is a fictional note-taking product used as demo data for RagForge. All plans, prices, and limits are invented.

## Which plans are available?

Orbitly Notes has three plans:

- **Free:** up to 3 workspaces, 500 notes, and 1 GB of attachments.
- **Pro:** 12 USD per user per month when billed monthly, or 120 USD per user per year when billed annually. Includes unlimited notes, 50 GB of attachments, version history, and offline mode.
- **Business:** 25 USD per user per month. Includes everything in Pro, plus single sign-on (SAML), audit logs, and priority support.

Students and non-profit organizations get a 50% discount on the Pro plan after verifying their status.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan automatically unless someone upgrades.

## Does Orbitly Notes work offline?

Offline mode is available on the Pro and Business plans in the desktop and mobile apps. Notes opened in the last 30 days are cached locally. Changes made offline are synchronized automatically when the device reconnects. If the same note is edited on two devices while offline, Orbitly keeps both versions and asks the user to choose one.

## How do I export my data?

Workspace owners can export all notes from Settings → Data → Export. Exports are available as Markdown files in a ZIP archive or as a single JSON file. Attachments are included in the ZIP export. Large exports are prepared in the background and a download link is sent by email; the link expires after 7 days.

## Which integrations are supported?

Orbitly Notes integrates with Slack, Google Calendar, GitHub, and Zapier. The GitHub integration links notes to issues and pull requests. A public REST API is available on the Business plan, with a limit of 600 requests per minute per workspace.

## What happens to deleted notes?

Deleted notes go to the trash and can be restored for 30 days. After 30 days they are permanently deleted. Business workspaces can extend trash retention to 180 days.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Owners can cancel at any time in Settings → Billing. The plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. Annual plans canceled within the first 14 days receive a full refund; after that, no partial refunds are issued.
